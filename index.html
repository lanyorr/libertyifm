About The Ministry

Liberty House International Faith Ministries exists to reveal the love, liberty and transforming power of Jesus Christ through biblical truth, fervent prayer, discipleship and compassionate humanitarian service.

Our Foundation

Founders

Liberty House International Faith Ministries is led by Bishop Paul Nnamdi Ijeoma and Nelly-Praise Ijeoma, devoted to preaching Christ and raising transformed lives.

Mission

To reveal the love, power and salvation of Jesus Christ through sound biblical teaching, fervent prayer, compassionate outreach and transformational discipleship.

Vision

To raise a generation rooted in faith, walking in the liberty of Christ and impacting nations through evangelism, holiness, love and humanitarian service.

Core Emphasis

  • Prayer, worship and the Word
  • Family and youth discipleship
  • Evangelism and community impact
